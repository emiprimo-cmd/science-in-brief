Privacy Policy

Introduction

This website respects your privacy and is committed to protecting any personal information that may be collected while you use the site.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it may be used, and the measures taken to protect user privacy.

By using this website, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information Collected

Information You Voluntarily Provide

Certain information may be collected when you voluntarily submit it through forms or direct communication, including:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Messages or inquiries submitted through contact forms

This information is only collected when voluntarily provided by the user.

Automatically Collected Information

Like most websites, this site may automatically collect limited technical information, such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device information
  • Pages visited
  • Date and time of access
  • Referring website links

This information is generally used for analytics, website performance, and security purposes.

Article reaction buttons may store reaction counts locally in your browser using localStorage. These local reactions are not a site-wide vote total and are not transmitted by this website.

Cookies & Analytics

This website may use cookies or similar technologies to improve user experience and analyze website traffic.

Third-party services such as analytics providers may collect anonymous usage data through cookies.

Users can disable cookies through their browser settings if preferred.

How Information Is Used

Collected information may be used to:

  • Respond to inquiries or messages
  • Improve website performance and content
  • Analyze website traffic and visitor behavior
  • Maintain website security and functionality

Personal information is not sold or intentionally shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

This website may contain links to external websites, scientific articles, social media platforms, or third-party services.

Services currently used or linked may include Buttondown for newsletter subscriptions, Google Fonts for web fonts, DOI providers for source papers, GitHub for article discussion links, LinkedIn, and ORCID.

Please note that external websites operate under their own privacy policies, and this website is not responsible for their practices or content.

Data Protection

Reasonable measures are taken to protect submitted information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children’s Privacy

This website is not intended for children under the age required by applicable privacy regulations. No personal information is knowingly collected from children.

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in website practices, legal requirements, or technical updates.

The latest version will always be available on this page.

Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of personal information, please contact the website owner through the contact page.
